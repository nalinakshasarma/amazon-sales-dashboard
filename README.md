# Amazon India Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Amazon India sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify sales trends, evaluate business performance, and generate actionable insights through an interactive dashboard.

The analysis is based on **10,000 sales records** and uses PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, and data analysis techniques to explore different aspects of business performance.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel 2019
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel Slicers
- Data Cleaning and Data Analysis
- Interactive Dashboard Design

## Business Objectives
The project addresses four key business objectives:

1. **Overall Sales & Profit Performance** – Analyze total sales, profit, order volume, units sold, average order value, and profit margin.
2. **Category & Product Performance** – Identify top-performing product categories and products based on sales.
3. **Order Status Analysis** – Evaluate delivered, shipped, cancelled, and returned orders.
4. **Payment, Fulfillment & Geographic Analysis** – Examine sales and profit by payment method, fulfillment method, and state.

## Key Insights
- Electronics & Mobiles contributed approximately ₹12.36 Crore in sales, making it the leading category.
- 81.47% of orders were delivered, while 9.85% were returned or cancelled.
- Amazon FBA accounted for approximately 69% of fulfillment sales.
- Maharashtra recorded the highest sales at approximately ₹2.96 Crore, with 1,840 orders.
- UPI generated the highest profit among payment methods, at approximately ₹1.67 Crore.

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate product quality, packaging, and customer concerns to reduce returns in Electronics & Mobiles.
- Review cancellation patterns in Apparel & Fashion.
- Strengthen Amazon FBA operations and maintain efficient inventory levels.
- Maintain strong performance in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Delhi while exploring growth opportunities in other states.
